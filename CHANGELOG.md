# Changelog

## 9.0.0

- **Expeditions.** Send a team of up to three on a Mystery Dungeon-style
  expedition: pick one of 9 places (Misty Forest, Crystal Cave, Coral Reef,
  Mt. Blaze, Old Power Plant, Toxic Marsh, Frozen Peak, Forgotten Ruins,
  Sky Tower) and a difficulty (Easy 30 min to Expert 4 h) in a planning window.
  Then follow the team in the popup: it walks down a Mystery Dungeon corridor
  floor by floor, with HP / PP / fullness, the provisions left and a log of
  everything that happened.
- **Real battles.** Wild Pokémon from the whole National Dex, 0 to 5 levels under
  your team, fight with their real stats, abilities and level-up moves. Damage
  follows the Pokémon Showdown calculator: STAB, types, crits, weather, stat
  stages, status conditions, multi-hit, recoil, drain and many abilities.
- **Events.** Some 50 things can happen down there: traps, treasure chests,
  Kecleon shops, Kangaskhan Storage, campfires, quarrels between natures,
  Monster Houses, rival teams, floor bosses… and, rarely, a legendary.
- **Provisions.** Pack apples, berries, potions, Ethers, cures, Revives and an
  Escape Rope. The team uses them on its own when it needs them.
- **Recruits and legendaries.** A defeated wild Pokémon may want to join (catch
  rate based). Legendaries live in the places that suit them, show up from Normal
  (young ones), Hard or Expert, and only offer to join by luck, from the second
  time you meet them in that place.
- **Exploration rank.** Rank points unlock places, difficulties, bigger bags
  and up to 3 teams at once. Teams can be called back; they come home with all
  their loot. A defeated team loses half of what it found.
- **Poké Balls.** Balls are now items: Poké, Great (×1.5), Ultra (×2) and
  Master Balls. Catching uses the main-series formula, tuned so hard-to-catch
  Pokémon stay catchable even though you can't weaken them. Everybody gets 10
  Poké Balls to start.
- **Search.** One search per half hour, which shows everything you found at
  once: 1 to 5 Poké Balls (sometimes Great or Ultra Balls, very rarely a Master
  Ball) and 5 items.
- **Menu.** Click the title of the popup for a phone-like home screen: partner,
  Pokédex, Bag, Shop, Day Care, Training, Expeditions, their history and the
  Summary, each with its counter.
- **Shop.** Spend Poké Dollars on Balls, provisions, candies, mints and
  evolution items (mints and rare evolution items change every day), or sell
  items back at half price. Expeditions pay ₽ for every wild Pokémon defeated,
  floor explored and boss beaten (more on harder ones, +25% when cleared, half
  when defeated). Everybody starts with ₽300.
- **Day Care.** It now looks after 10 Pokémon, who earn 12 XP an hour; the
  others rest without XP. Choose who goes from the Day Care screen or a summary.
  Saves keep their 10 most advanced Pokémon there.
- **Apples.** Every search finds 1 to 3 apples: Apple, Big Apple or, rarely, a
  Golden Apple, as in Mystery Dungeon. Expeditions find them too.
- Legendaries and rare Pokémon show up less often in the tall grass.
- Pokémon on an expedition don't play at the Day Care or train; your partner can go.

## 8.0.0

- **Boxes.** Catch the same Pokémon more than once: each family has a box of up
  to 32 Pokémon. Click a Pokédex entry to open it, and from there view each
  Pokémon's summary, send it to the Day Care, release it or manage its moves.
- **Nav-Dex.** Lock wild encounters on any species you've seen, to hunt a better
  one or a shiny.
- **Shiny Charm.** Catching every family doubles the shiny odds (1/64 → 1/32).
- **Moves.** Pokémon learn their level-up moves as they grow and when they evolve.
  The Move Reminder brings back any move they know.
- **Incense.** A new item: 10 wild encounters per half hour instead of 3, for 1h30.
- Item searches go from 1 to 5 per half hour.
- The Pokédex shows the families you've seen but not caught yet.
- Long names no longer overlap the level in the popup header.
- Older saves are upgraded automatically.
